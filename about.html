About us

A care company built around people, not just conditions.

Samara Health Care LLP was established in 2026 to give elders and recovering patients a place between hospital and home — where medical care and compassionate living come together.

Our story

Between hospital and home, a place that cares.

Many families face the same difficult moment: a loved one is ready to leave hospital, but not yet ready to manage at home. Samara was created for that moment — with trained nurses, careful records and the warmth of a family.

Our mission

To provide safe, respectful and reliable care that helps every resident live with comfort and dignity, and keeps families informed at every step.

Our vision

To be a trusted name in elder care and post-hospital recovery in Tamil Nadu, known for compassion, clinical safety and transparency.

Our values

  • Compassion — we treat every resident as family.
  • Dignity — privacy, choice and respect in daily care.
  • Safety — clinical standards and careful records.
  • Honesty — clear communication and transparent billing.

Leadership

Samara Health Care LLP is led by its two directors, who are closely involved in the care, quality and day-to-day running of the centre.

Dr. Krishnan Chellammal

Director, Samara Health Care LLP

Fondly known as Mrs. Chella Boomi

A lifetime devoted to nursing — and to the people it serves.

Dr. Krishnan Chellammal is a senior nursing and healthcare leader whose career spans more than 35 years of clinical care, nursing administration and hospital management.

Over these years, she has held senior leadership positions in some of the most respected healthcare institutions in India and abroad — Apollo Hospitals, Apollo Proton Cancer Centre, Kauvery Hospital, Kovai Medical Center and Hospital (KMCH), Sri Ramachandra Medical College (SRMC), Dr. Mehta’s Hospitals and King Fahd Specialist Hospital.

As Chief Nursing Officer and Group Head – Nursing at KMCH, she led a team of nearly 1,500 nursing professionals across the group. Earlier, as Nursing Director and Chief Nursing Officer at Kauvery Hospital, Chennai, she helped build its nursing services from the ground up, beginning at the project stage.

She holds an M.Sc. (Nursing), an MBA in Health Care Services from Anna University and a Ph.D. in Hospital Management from Bharathiar University. Her contribution to the profession has been recognised with the Excellence in Nursing Award from the Association of Healthcare Providers (India) – AHPI.

At Samara, she brings these decades of experience to a single purpose — care that is safe, compassionate and dignified, where professional healthcare standards meet the warmth and personal attention of a caring environment.

Dr. Maneesha Boominathan

Director, Samara Health Care LLP
