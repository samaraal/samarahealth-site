About us

A care company built around people, not just conditions.

Samara Health Care LLP was established in 2026 to give elders and recovering patients a place between hospital and home — where medical care and compassionate living come together.

Our mission

To provide safe, respectful and reliable care that helps every resident live with comfort and dignity, and keeps families informed at every step.

Our vision

To be a trusted name in elder care and post-hospital recovery in Tamil Nadu, known for compassion, clinical safety and transparency.

Our values

  • Compassion — we treat every resident as family.
  • Dignity — privacy, choice and respect in daily care.
  • Safety — clinical standards and careful records.
  • Honesty — clear communication and transparent billing.
