Our care

When home care is no longer enough — but hospital care is not required.

At Samara Assisted Living, a single integrated team of nurses, caregivers, physiotherapists and visiting doctors plans care around each person.

24×7 skilled nursing

Round-the-clock trained nurses for monitoring, wound care, feeding support and clinical procedures.

Recovery after hospital

Step-down care after surgery, stroke, fractures or long illness — a safe bridge from hospital to home.

Medication safety

Medicines given on time as prescribed, with every dose recorded and reviewed.

Doctor coordination

Regular vitals, doctor visits and coordination with the resident's own doctors and hospitals.

Physiotherapy & rehabilitation

Planned sessions to rebuild strength, balance and mobility, with progress shared with family.

Diet & nutrition

Meals planned for each resident's health needs, with intake recorded daily.

Daily living & companionship

Gentle help with bathing, dressing, toileting and moving around, plus warm company every day.

Short stays & family respite

Flexible stays when families travel, need a break, or are planning longer-term care.

Who may benefit

  • Elders living alone who need daily support
  • Patients discharged from hospital who still need nursing care
  • People recovering from stroke, surgery or fractures
  • Families who need a safe short stay or respite

Comfortable living

Private, twin-sharing and triple-sharing rooms, with flexible package types. For current rooms and packages, please visit the Samara Assisted Living website or call us.
