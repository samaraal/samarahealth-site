Privacy notice

How we handle the details you share

This notice explains how Samara Health Care LLP ("Samara", "we") uses the details you give us through this website, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form: the name and age of the person who needs care, your name, your mobile number, the type of care and any message you add.

Why we use it

To contact you about your enquiry and to help plan care. We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated advertising.

Where it is kept

In Samara's secure care management system. Only authorised Samara staff handling enquiries can see it.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for your enquiry and any care that follows, or as required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us to show, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, at any time by calling or messaging us on the numbers on the Contact page.

Not for emergencies

This website is not monitored for emergencies. In a medical emergency, call 108.
